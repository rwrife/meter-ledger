Meter Ledger keeps manual household utility readings in a local, offline SQLite journal for Windows, Android, and iOS phones and tablets. Add properties and electricity, water, or gas meters; enter dated cumulative readings and corrections with reasons; record billed intervals and optional paid totals. Review unit-labeled dated usage and bill differences. Missing exact boundary readings and meter replacements show unknown, never an estimate. Documented display rollover requires a configured modulus. Export readings as CSV or back up the journal as JSON; import replaces local data only after validation and confirmation. Navigation and date controls adapt to each platform. No provider connection, accounts, cloud sync, automatic leak detection, tariff prediction, or guaranteed savings.
