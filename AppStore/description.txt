Meter Ledger is planned as a private, offline household utility journal for Windows, Android, and iOS. Record electricity, water, or gas meter readings; compare dated readings with billed intervals; and see where a bill lacks enough readings for a reliable comparison. Keep property and meter histories locally and export your own records. Manual-entry utility log only: no provider connection, automatic leak detection, or guaranteed savings. Development status: documentation and artwork scaffold; app features not yet implemented.
