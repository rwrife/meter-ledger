Meter Ledger keeps manual household utility readings in a local, offline SQLite journal for Windows, Android, and iOS. Add properties and electricity, water, or gas meters; enter dated cumulative readings and corrections with reasons; record billed intervals and optional paid totals. Review unit-labeled dated usage and bill differences. Missing exact boundary readings and meter replacements show unknown, never an estimate. Documented display rollover requires a configured modulus. No provider connection, accounts, cloud sync, automatic leak detection, tariff prediction, or guaranteed savings. Backup/export, polished platform-adaptive navigation and release packaging are planned, not shipped.
